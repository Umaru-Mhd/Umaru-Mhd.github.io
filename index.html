Umaru Mohammed

Doctoral Researcher, Future Ecologies Group Lab, School of Design, Southern University of Science and Technology

Towards adaptive, nature-based buildings for healthier and low-carbon indoor environments.

Future Ecologies Group / Google Scholar / ORCID / GitHub

I am a doctoral researcher in the Future Ecologies Group Lab at SUSTech, working at the intersection of biophilic design, adaptive buildings, IAQ/IEQ, building performance simulation, IoT sensing, and low-carbon built environments. My PhD work explores how nature-based design strategies and intelligent building systems can support healthier indoor environments while reducing energy demand and operational carbon.

I previously lectured in Building Engineering at Imam Abdulrahman Bin Faisal University in Dammam, Saudi Arabia, with academic interests in sustainable building design, building performance, digital workflows, and evidence-led environmental analysis.

Research interests

Biophilic and nature-based design for adaptive buildings; IAQ and IEQ; thermal comfort; daylight performance; CFD airflow analysis; building performance simulation; nZEB pathways; IoT-supported sensing; indoor PV energy harvesting; and low-carbon sustainable built environments.
